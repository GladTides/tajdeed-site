Tajdeed: Renewal Reminders

Document expiry for the family. Arabic, English, French and Spanish.

I'm not getting reminders

Open iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then Tajdeed, and turn on Allow Notifications. Reminders arrive at 9:00 in the morning on the days you chose.

I can't hear the voice reminder

Voice reminders play only when your iPhone isn't on silent or in a Focus mode. For a more natural voice, download an Enhanced voice in Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content, then Voices.

The scan didn't find the date

Hold the card flat in good light so the expiry date is sharp. You can always type the date yourself, in Hijri or Gregorian.

Moving to a new iPhone

In Settings, tap Save backup file and save it to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new iPhone, tap Restore from file.

I already paid on another iPhone

Tap Restore purchase in Settings while signed in with the same Apple ID.
