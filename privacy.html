Privacy policy

Tajdeed: Renewal Reminders · Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version

Tajdeed does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Document details you type (type, name, owner, number, expiry date, reminders, notes) are saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone.
  • Photos of documents you choose to keep are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • Expiry dates are read from photos with Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo is never uploaded.
  • Spoken reminders are created on your iPhone with Apple's built-in voices.

Backups

When you choose "Save backup file", the app creates a file and opens the iPhone share sheet. You decide where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, WhatsApp or elsewhere). Tajdeed never sends it anywhere on its own.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple. Tajdeed never sees your payment details.

Permissions

Camera and photos are used only when you scan or attach a document. Notifications are used only for your reminders. Face ID is used only to lock the app if you turn that on.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Contact

Questions: support@scanzest.uk
